# mail-snail draft artwork

A pet app by Astra. Generated with the built-in image tool, using the Cage family style, by OpenAI Codex (GPT-6), 2026-10-11, America/Denver. Status: draft, awaiting owner review.

## Generation prompt

Create a 3x3 grid sprite sheet of the SAME original pixel-art desktop pet shown exactly nine times, one complete character per evenly spaced square cell. VERY FINE crisp pixel art with small pixels, polished SNES-era detail, about 80 art pixels tall per sprite. Flat solid colors with a few discrete shade blocks, crisp hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no dithering, no 3D. A solid thin BLACK pixel outline traces every silhouette. Tiny dot eyes with one white highlight, small pink blush cheeks, cute and friendly. Same body, same proportions, same palette, same size and centered pose in every cell; ONLY facial expression changes. Row-major mood order: neutral calm, eyes closed/blink, happy smile; curious, worried, sad; unwell, shocked, asleep. Curious uses a raised brow and little open mouth; eyes remain symmetric and face forward. Every sprite fully visible with generous transparent padding, no clipping, no overlaps. Actual transparent alpha background, not black/white/gray or checkerboard. No grid lines, borders, letters, numbers, words, logos, shadows or watermarks. Do not draw question marks, exclamation marks, Z, tears, sweat or bandages; the app composites mood symbols later.

Character specification: Mail Snail is a cute plump little snail with a distinctive vivid CORAL spiral shell and soft MINT body with a CREAM underside. Two upright little eye stalks, friendly tiny highlighted dark eyes and blush. A simple small cream envelope motif with a plain diagonal fold is visible on the front lower part of the coral shell like a mail pouch; it contains NO text, letters, logos or stamps. Three-quarter front view so friendly face and spiral shell are both readable; same view every mood. Compact smooth snail silhouette, no human arms or hands, no legs. A tiny mouth supplies the clear expressions; asleep and blink close eyes on stalks.

## Targeted correction prompt

Precise-object-edit of this transparent 3x3 Mail Snail pixel-art sheet. Correct ONLY the duplicate eyes in all nine cells: the snail must have exactly TWO eyes total, both at the ends of its two tall eye stalks. Remove every small black eye or eyebrow from the lower round face and fill those tiny areas with the surrounding matching mint skin. Retain the two pink blush spots and little mouth in each cell. Retain all nine eye-stalk expressions and mouth expressions, all original cell positioning, coral spiral shell, cream belly, small envelope, mint body, proportions, shading and black pixel outline. Keep transparent background. Do not add anything. No extra face eyes beneath the stalks. Every cell should have two stalk eyes plus a mouth and blush only.
